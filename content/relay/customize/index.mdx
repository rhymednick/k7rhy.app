These are optional changes you can make to a Relay guitar, either one you've finished or one you're partway through building. You don't need any of them to build a great guitar. They're here for when you want it to behave a little differently.

## Alternative tone wiring

The first one is an **alternative tone wiring**. It adds a three-way switch with Direct, Off, and Reactive positions to the tone circuit. It needs one drilled hole near the tone pot, and nothing else on the body changes.

I'd start with the background, then move on to the switch itself. Each is a short series, so you can read one part at a time and stop wherever you've got what you need:

- **Understand** is three short parts: [why turning down makes it dull](/guitars/relay/customize/treble-bleed/understand/why-turning-down-dulls), what a treble bleed does about it, and how a reactive bleed follows the tone knob.
- **Build** is five short parts: [is your guitar a fit](/guitars/relay/customize/treble-bleed/build/is-it-a-fit), the parts and the hole, what changes and finding your lugs, the wiring and its checks, and a reference sheet with troubleshooting.
