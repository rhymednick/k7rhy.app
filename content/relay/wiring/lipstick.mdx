---
title: 'Relay Lipstick Wiring'
description: 'Relay Lipstick pickup, selector, control, and grounding connections.'
---

The Relay Lipstick uses a three-way selector for the bridge and neck humbuckers. The volume push-push adds the middle lipstick pickup in parallel and partially splits the bridge. The tone control is a conventional A500k pot with a 22 nF capacitor.
