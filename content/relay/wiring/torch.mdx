Relay Torch uses a five-way blade for its GFS VEH bridge humbucker, Mean 90 middle pickup, and Professional Series Alnico II neck humbucker. An A500k master volume controls the output without a treble bleed. The A500k push-pull tone pot provides a standard modern 22 nF tone branch and switches the global Edge Contour.

| Tone push-pull | Signal path after the five-way selector                  | Result                                              |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| Down           | Selector output directly to volume input                 | Full, unshaped pickup signal.                       |
| Pulled up      | Selector output through the Edge network to volume input | Tighter lows and a more prominent upper-mid attack. |

The **Edge network** is a 2.2 nF (`0.0022 µF`, `222`) capacitor **in parallel** with a 150 kΩ resistor. Those two components form one network that is switched **in series** with the selected pickup signal. It affects every blade position. This passive contour removes some low-frequency energy relative to the highs; it does not add gain.

The standard tone branch takes its feed from the **volume input**, after the Edge switch, and runs through the A500k tone pot and 22 nF (`0.022 µF`, `223`) capacitor to ground. This is modern master-tone wiring. The tone feed does not come from the volume wiper, and the tone pot is not a no-load control.

## Connection map

The labels below describe electrical nodes. They are **functional contact names**, not physical lug numbers for an arbitrary switch. Check the actual blade and push-pull with a continuity meter before soldering.

| Node          | Connect these points                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `B`, `M`, `N` | Bridge, middle, and neck pickup hot leads to their respective five-way selector inputs. The selector connects them to its common in the five positions above.                        |
| `SEL`         | Five-way common output; direct/down switch throw; one end of both Edge components.                                                                                                   |
| `EDGE`        | Join the other ends of the 2.2 nF capacitor and 150 kΩ resistor; connect that joint to the pulled/up switch throw.                                                                   |
| `BUS`         | Push-pull switch common; A500k volume input; A500k tone input. Down joins `SEL` to `BUS`; pulled up joins `EDGE` to `BUS`.                                                           |
| `OUT`         | Volume wiper to mono output-jack tip. No treble-bleed component bridges `BUS` and `OUT`.                                                                                             |
| Tone branch   | `BUS` to tone pot CCW end; tone wiper through the 22 nF capacitor to `GND`. Leave the tone pot CW end unused.                                                                        |
| `GND`         | Pickup returns and separate shields/cases, grounded volume-track end, tone-cap return, pot cases, conductive selector chassis, cavity shield, bridge/string ground, and jack sleeve. |

**Pot orientation:** In the rear view with shafts pointing away and solder lugs down, the physical left/center/right terminals are CW/wiper/CCW. The volume's left/center/right lugs are `BUS`/`OUT`/`GND`. The tone's left/center/right lugs are unused/capacitor/`BUS`. Both knobs should brighten or increase level when turned clockwise.

**Pickup leads:** Use the supplied guide for each specific GFS lead version and confirm with a meter; wire colors are not interchangeable across pickup models. Each humbucker stays in full series. Insulate an unused single split lead, or join and insulate two separate series ends when the pickup supplies those instead. Keep a separate case or shield lead grounded. The Mean 90 hot feeds `M` and its return and shield go to `GND` as supplied.

## Build outline

1. Identify the actual five-way contacts and the push-pull common and throws by continuity. Confirm the switch makes a direct `SEL`–`BUS` path down and an `SEL`–Edge network–`BUS` path pulled up.
2. Wire each pickup hot to its blade input and complete the `GND` network. Build the A500k volume, modern A500k tone branch, 22 nF cap, and output jack.
3. Wire the 2.2 nF and 150 kΩ Edge components in parallel and connect the two switch paths. Insulate the unused push-pull pole and all unused pickup series-junction leads.
4. Test the complete harness in every selector and push-pull combination before installing it in the guitar.

## Bench checks

- Meter the five-way selector: `SEL` must connect only to bridge, bridge + middle, middle, neck + middle, or neck in the listed positions. Verify both push-pull contact states separately.
- With the volume full up, confirm no near-zero-ohm short from jack tip to sleeve. At volume zero, the tip should reach ground. DC resistance varies with pickup and pot tolerances; measure the isolated pickups rather than using fixed advertised resistances as pass/fail limits.
- Tap-test all ten selector/contour states at low amplifier volume. The contour should affect whichever pickup selection is active; it must not add a pickup or change the five-way map.
- Turn both knobs from stop to stop. Clockwise should increase volume and brighten the tone. Listen to all pickup combinations for unexpected cancellation and verify that rolling down volume cleans up without a treble-bleed network.
