Relay Lipstick, Relay Torch, and Relay Velvet all specify a 22 nF (`0.022 µF`, `223`) master-tone capacitor. Relay Reef Plus needs **two** 22 nF capacitors, one for each side's tone. The Torch Edge Contour also needs a **separate** 2.2 nF (`222`) capacitor and 150 kΩ resistor, wired together as shown in its wiring guide. Do not use the 2.2 nF contour capacitor in place of the 22 nF tone capacitor.
