## Design intent

Coupeville Current begins with three familiar pickup selections: neck, both humbuckers, and bridge. Those selections remain the primary voices. A six-position rotary switch routes the middle Filtertron pickup through engineered presets, using it as a harmonic shaper for the selected voice.

This arrangement separates pickup selection from harmonic shaping. The player can move through progressively lighter interaction or bypass the shaper without changing the active pickup selection.

## Passive interaction

The middle GFS Retrotron Hot Nashville is dedicated to the harmonic shaper. Positions 1 through 5 place different passive networks in its path, changing how it interacts with the selected humbucker voice. Position 6 disconnects the middle pickup, so the selected voice passes unchanged.

The harmonic shaper is therefore separate from pickup selection. The three-way switch answers which humbucker voice is active; the six-position rotary switch determines how the middle Filtertron interacts with it.

## Installed electrical reference

The master volume and master tone are A500K Audio controls. The tone circuit uses a 22 nF capacitor. A 680 pF capacitor and 150 kΩ resistor form the treble bleed and are wired in parallel across the master-volume input and output.

The numbered technical references in the control map document the installed passive network at each harmonic shaper position. They correspond directly to the six positions on the instrument.

## Builder Notes

**Strings:** 9–42 is the recommended set. 10–46 is the maximum recommended string weight for the as-delivered setup.

**Amplifier pairing:** Begin with an amplifier setting that preserves pick attack and provides enough clean headroom to compare the six shaping positions. From there, set gain and equalization for the musical context and playing level.
