## The guitar

This is a natural-finish PRS SE Custom 24 Burled Ash Limited Edition, made in 2024. It has a burled swamp-ash veneer over a maple top, a mahogany back, a Wide Thin maple neck, and a rosewood fingerboard with bird inlays, 24 frets, and a 25-inch scale. The hardware is nickel, with a PRS molded tremolo. As part of the K7RHY rework, the original tuners were replaced with PRS locking tuners.

## A platform-reference instrument

While other K7RHY guitars were being designed, assembled, and evaluated, this was the platform-reference instrument: the guitar the others were compared against. Its consistent feel and wide range of switched voices made it a dependable baseline for dynamic response, pickup balance, control utility, and clean-to-edge-of-breakup voicing.

## How it plays

The Alnico II Pro pickups give the guitar a warm, touch-sensitive center. The two controls work like any two-knob guitar, and the five-way selector moves from bridge to neck in order.

Positions 1 and 5 are the full humbuckers, with the weight for stronger rhythm parts and sustained leads. Positions 2, 3, and 4 use partial splits: one coil of each selected pickup is mostly bypassed, not cut off completely, so these voices sound open and quick without turning thin. Position 3 plays both partly split pickups together.

Pulling the tone knob up takes the tone control out of the circuit. Set the knob where you like it for a darker sound, then pull up for full brightness and push down to get your setting back, without reaching for the knob mid-song.

The guitar suits blues, funk, soul, folk, jazz, and yacht rock, and especially expressive playing that moves between clean and the edge of breakup, where changes in touch and guitar volume stay easy to hear.

## Electronics

The factory electronics were replaced with a Seymour Duncan APH-1b/APH-1n Alnico II Pro set, both mounted with the screw coil toward the bridge. The selector is an Oak Grigsby four-pole five-way super switch. The 500 kΩ master volume has a 180 pF treble-bleed capacitor. The 500 kΩ push-pull master tone uses a 0.022 µF film capacitor, which the push-pull disconnects when pulled up. The control cavity is shielded with copper foil.

Each partial split bridges one coil of a pickup with a 1.8 kΩ resistor: the bridge pickup in positions 2 and 3, the neck pickup in positions 3 and 4.
