## The guitar

This is a natural-finish PRS SE Custom 24 Burled Ash Limited Edition, made in 2024. It has a burled swamp-ash veneer over a maple top, a mahogany back, a Wide Thin maple neck, and a rosewood fingerboard with bird inlays, 24 frets, and a 25-inch scale. The hardware is nickel, with a PRS molded tremolo. As part of the K7RHY rework, the original tuners were replaced with PRS locking tuners.

## A platform-reference instrument

While other K7RHY guitars were being designed, assembled, and evaluated, this was the platform-reference instrument: the guitar the others were compared against. Its consistent feel and wide range of switched voices made it a dependable baseline for dynamic response, pickup balance, control utility, and clean-to-edge-of-breakup voicing.

## How it plays

The Alnico II Pro pickups give the guitar a warm, touch-sensitive center. The two controls work like any two-knob guitar, and the five-way selector moves from bridge to neck in order.

Positions 1, 3, and 5 are full humbucker sounds with the weight for stronger rhythm parts and sustained leads. Positions 2 and 4 use partial splits: one coil of each selected pickup is mostly bypassed, not cut off completely, so these voices sound open and quick without turning thin. Position 2 pairs the two coils nearest the middle of the guitar, and those two coils cancel hum.

Pulling the tone knob up gives three more voices. Positions 1, 3, and 5 become clearer and lower in output while still canceling hum, which suits clean playing and riding the volume knob into light breakup. Positions 2 and 4 stay the same, so the selector behaves predictably in either mode.

Together, the eight voices suit blues, funk, soul, folk, jazz, and yacht rock, and especially expressive playing that moves between clean and the edge of breakup, where changes in touch and guitar volume stay easy to hear.

## Electronics

The factory electronics were replaced with a Seymour Duncan APH-1b/APH-1n Alnico II Pro set, both mounted with the screw coil toward the bridge. The selector is an Oak Grigsby four-pole five-way super switch. The controls are a 500 kΩ master volume and a 500 kΩ push-pull master tone with a 0.022 µF film capacitor.

Each partial split bridges one coil with a 1.8 kΩ resistor. The bridge split keeps the bridge slug coil; the neck split keeps the neck screw coil. Both are the inside coils, nearest the middle of the guitar. The push-pull's two poles change every selected humbucker between series and parallel, and are not used in the split positions.
