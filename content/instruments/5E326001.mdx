## The amplifier

This individually built combo is based on the late-1950s Fender 5E3 circuit. Its two channels have separate volume controls and a shared Tone control. The Normal and Bright Volume controls interact even when only one channel is in use: the unused channel's setting can change gain, tone, and response.

The installed Electro-Harmonix speaker is rated at 75 watts and 8 ohms. The speaker rating is distinct from the amplifier's approximately 12 watts of output. Its cable is soldered at the speaker and plugs into the amplifier's speaker-output jack; check that plug before operation.

## Operating and service documentation

The amplifier was supplied with a completed Build Verification Record, as-shipped operating-voltage measurements, grounding and electrical-isolation test results, a Quick Start and Controls guide, and a High-voltage Safety Guide. Keep these amplifier-specific documents with the unit for future servicing.

These are builder-performed inspection and measurement records, not independent safety certification. Follow the supplied operating guide for the Power and Standby sequence.

## Safe use

Remove every piece of packing material from the rear cabinet cavity before connecting power. Operate only from a properly grounded outlet, with the speaker connected. Tubes become extremely hot during use.

Potentially lethal voltages and stored electrical charge are present inside the chassis. Do not open it or attempt internal service unless trained to work safely on high-voltage tube amplifiers.
