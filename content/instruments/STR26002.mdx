## The instrument

The white poplar body and maple neck are paired with a black pickguard and Guyker locking tuners. New Fender CuNiFe Stratocaster pickups, sourced directly from Fender, give the guitar its defining voice.

The five-way selector provides the familiar S-type choices. The neck-add switch brings the neck pickup into the selected sound in parallel, opening up bridge-and-neck and all-three-pickup combinations without complicating the main controls.

## Controls and switching

Master volume and tone work in every pickup setting. The volume control is designed to retain clarity when turned down. The five-way selector and neck-add switch provide these combinations:

1. **Bridge:** bridge alone; bridge and neck with neck-add on.
2. **Bridge and middle:** bridge and middle; all three pickups with neck-add on.
3. **Middle:** middle alone; middle and neck with neck-add on.
4. **Middle and neck:** unchanged by the neck-add switch.
5. **Neck:** unchanged by the neck-add switch.

## Electronics

The A250K audio-taper master volume has a treble-bleed network across its input and output: a 1,200 pF capacitor in parallel with a 150 kΩ resistor, with that pair in series with a 20 kΩ resistor. The A500K audio-taper master tone uses a 0.022 µF capacitor and works in every pickup setting.

A standard five-way selector selects the pickups. A latching SPST on-off micro switch in the third control position adds the neck pickup in parallel with the selected sound.
