## The instrument

The white poplar body and maple neck are paired with a black pickguard, tremolo bridge, and Guyker locking tuners. Fender CuNiFe Stratocaster pickups, sourced directly from Fender, give the guitar its defining voice. It weighs 7 lb 2 oz (3.2 kg).

The five-way selector provides the familiar S-type choices. The neck-add switch brings the neck pickup into the selected sound in parallel, opening up bridge-and-neck and all-three-pickup combinations without complicating the main controls.

## What it is voiced to do

Fender describes the CuNiFe set as a vintage-style voice with deep, piano-like lows, a present midrange, and sweet, sparkling highs that keep their clarity and definition under heavy distortion. The middle pickup is reverse-wound, so the two in-between positions cancel hum. The neck-add switch opens two combinations a standard five-way cannot reach. Bridge and neck together pair the bridge's definition with the neck's body; all three pickups give the widest, most open blend.

## Electronics

The A250K audio-taper master volume has a treble-bleed network across its input and output: a 1,200 pF capacitor in parallel with a 150 kΩ resistor, with that pair in series with a 20 kΩ resistor. The A500K audio-taper master tone uses a 0.022 µF capacitor.

A standard five-way selector selects the pickups. A latching SPST on-off micro switch in the third control position adds the neck pickup in parallel with the selected sound.
