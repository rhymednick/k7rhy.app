## The instrument

The white poplar body and maple neck are paired with a bamboo pickguard and Guyker locking tuners. New Fender CuNiFe Stratocaster pickups, sourced directly from Fender, give the guitar its defining voice.

The five-way selector keeps the familiar S-type sounds close at hand. A small switch adds a series option in positions 2 and 4, giving the adjacent-pickup pairs a fuller, more assertive voice when a part calls for it. The three single-pickup positions stay the same.

## Controls and switching

Master volume and tone work in every pickup setting. The volume control is designed to retain clarity when turned down. The five-way selector and series switch provide these combinations:

1. **Bridge:** bridge pickup in either mode.
2. **Bridge and middle:** parallel in the standard setting; series in the series setting.
3. **Middle:** middle pickup in either mode.
4. **Middle and neck:** parallel in the standard setting; series in the series setting.
5. **Neck:** neck pickup in either mode.

## Electronics

The A250K audio-taper master volume has a treble-bleed network across its input and output: a 1,200 pF capacitor in parallel with a 150 kΩ resistor, with that pair in series with a 20 kΩ resistor. The A500K audio-taper master tone uses a 0.022 µF capacitor and works in every pickup setting.

A four-pole five-way super switch selects the pickups. A latching DPDT on-on micro switch in the third control position changes the bridge-and-middle and middle-and-neck combinations between parallel and series.
