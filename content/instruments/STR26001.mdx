## The instrument

The white poplar body and maple neck are paired with a bamboo pickguard and Guyker locking tuners. Fender CuNiFe Stratocaster pickups, sourced directly from Fender, give the guitar its defining voice.

The five-way selector keeps the familiar S-type sounds close at hand. A small switch adds a series option in positions 2 and 4, giving the adjacent-pickup pairs a fuller, more assertive voice when a part calls for it. The three single-pickup positions stay the same.

## What it is voiced to do

Fender describes the CuNiFe set as a vintage-style voice with deep, piano-like lows, a present midrange, and sweet, sparkling highs that keep their clarity and definition under heavy distortion. The middle pickup is reverse-wound, so the two in-between positions cancel hum. Series mode joins the same pairs end to end instead of side by side. The result is louder and thicker, with more midrange push, while still canceling hum: a stronger voice for leads and driven parts without leaving the familiar layout.

## Electronics

The A250K audio-taper master volume has a treble-bleed network across its input and output: a 1,200 pF capacitor in parallel with a 150 kΩ resistor, with that pair in series with a 20 kΩ resistor. The A500K audio-taper master tone uses a 0.022 µF capacitor.

A four-pole five-way super switch selects the pickups. A latching DPDT on-on micro switch in the third control position changes the bridge-and-middle and middle-and-neck combinations between parallel and series.
