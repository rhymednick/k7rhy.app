## Extraction notes

- This correction supersedes only the intended `VELVET-ERP-V1` pickup loadout.
- The GM ’59 / Nashville / GM ’59 trio remains valid diagnostic Prototype 1 history and measured baseline evidence.
- No individual pickup is production-approved. The PRS Dragon II Bass is only the primary neck audition candidate; middle and bridge models remain unselected.
- Existing control-network and treble-bleed values remain bench starting points until revalidated with the selected replacement trio.

**Evidence:** Corrected

**Proposed classification:** Design decision

### VPTC-002 — Preserve measured GM baselines

**Statement:** Preserve the GM neck measurement of 3.59 H / Q 2.23 and bridge measurement of 5.09 H / Q 2.46 at 1 kHz as diagnostic evidence.

**Evidence:** Observed

**Proposed classification:** Serialized-instrument documentation

### VPTC-003 — Replace the convergent pickup set

**Statement:** Seek replacements because the installed set converged on broad, warm, moderate-Q humbucker behavior and height adjustment did not create enough clean separation.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Design decision

### VPTC-004 — Contrast precedes voice network and breakup

**Statement:** Require three meaningfully different clean voices before the global voice network or amplifier distortion is applied.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Platform, model, or voicing documentation

### VPTC-005 — Neck role and screening target

**Statement:** Seek a smooth, buttery neck voice with moderate output, controlled bass, rounded attack, harmonic detail, and clean separation, initially screening around 3.5–4.8 H and Q 2.5 or higher at 1 kHz.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Engineering recommendation

### VPTC-006 — Dragon II Bass is only a neck audition candidate

**Statement:** Treat the PRS Dragon II Bass as the primary neck audition candidate, advancing only if measurement and direct audition beat the GM neck for richness and separation.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Design decision

### VPTC-007 — Middle role and screening target

**Statement:** Seek a lower-inductance, higher-Q contrast voice with quick attack, upper-harmonic complexity, string definition, and useful blends, initially screening around 1.5–3.2 H and Q 2.7 or higher.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Engineering recommendation

### VPTC-008 — Nashville retention is conditional

**Statement:** Retain the GFS Nashville only if out-of-circuit measurement and simplified direct audition demonstrate the required contrast role.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Design decision

### VPTC-009 — Bridge role and screening target

**Statement:** Seek an authoritative bridge voice with firm bass, faster attack than the neck, harmonic and chord detail, smooth upper treble, and moderate output, initially screening around 2.8–4.2 H and Q 2.5 or higher.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Engineering recommendation

### VPTC-010 — Prefer verified Firebird construction for bridge audition

**Statement:** Prefer a true vintage-output Firebird-style bridge candidate, with clear low-output mini-humbucker or vintage-output Filter’Tron as secondary families, and verify construction rather than trusting the product label.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Design decision

### VPTC-011 — Select pickups as a system

**Statement:** Select the three pickups as a complementary electrical and musical system rather than as independently attractive products.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Engineering standard

**Notes:** The middle should generally have roughly 25–35% less inductance than the selected neck, but illustrative values are not specifications.

### VPTC-012 — Consistent out-of-circuit candidate record

**Statement:** Measure candidates with the same instrument and settings and record DCR, 1 kHz inductance and Q, construction, aperture, magnet when known, and intended position.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Engineering standard

### VPTC-013 — Two-load direct audition

**Statement:** Audition finalists direct to jack under 1 MΩ and approximately 500 kΩ reference loads and require articulation, useful smoothing, and resistance to muddy collapse.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Engineering standard

### VPTC-014 — Pickup height is for final balance

**Statement:** Use pickup height for final output balance, not to compensate for unsuitable basic pickup character.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Engineering standard

### VPTC-015 — Existing voice values remain starting points

**Statement:** Treat the documented global voice-network and treble-bleed values only as bench starting points for the replacement pickup set.

**Evidence:** Corrected

**Proposed classification:** Reference design

### VPTC-016 — Revalidate and retune the voice network

**Statement:** After selecting the replacement trio, repeat the five-position matrix and retune branches if any voice becomes redundant, blanketed, or too close to bypass.

**Evidence:** Confirmed

**Proposed classification:** Engineering standard

### VPTC-017 — Recompare master loading and treble bleeds
