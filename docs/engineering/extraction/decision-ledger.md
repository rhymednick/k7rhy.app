## Consolidation notes

- ZGDC-001, ZGDC-002, and ZGDC-003 overlap but are not duplicates: they separately establish authority, artifact persistence, and preservation of rationale.
- ZGDC-009 through ZGDC-020 are likely to inform one guitar-documentation standard, but no standard exists yet.
- ZGDC-021 and ZGDC-022 distinguish reusable design identity from a product's declaration of the revision it uses.
- ZGDC-023 defines classification boundaries and should be reconciled with the repository's existing content terminology before promotion.
- ZGDC-028 supersedes the earlier sequence that designed governance before harvesting conversations.
- CVPC-002 through CVPC-008 define one Coupeville Velvet build and should not become cross-product requirements. CVPC-007 records the intended 2.2 kΩ neck and 3.3 kΩ bridge partial splits, which have not yet been wired or validated by listening.
- CVPC-011 through CVPC-013 preserve the saved Harmonic Shaper intent and starting values; CVPC-014 through CVPC-017 preserve the later electrical and physical corrections needed to build it honestly.
- CVPC-014 and CVPC-016 supersede the earlier claim that a passive shunt could affect only the humbucker contribution after its output shares a node with the Nashville.
- CVPC-017 supersedes the position-dependent split-resistor proposal; CVPC-018 leaves unused rotary poles physically unpopulated and unassigned rather than treating them as a feature requirement.
- CVPC-020 records the component combinations already built into Velvet's Harmonic Shaper; the build cannot yet be tested, so tonal descriptions must remain provisional.
- CVPC-021 records historical diagnostic context rather than an open diagnosis; CRL-020 supersedes it by confirming that linear pots solved the bulk of the problem.
- RCCP-001 through RCCP-013 provide the strongest model-level source set so far for expanding the Coupeville Current page.
- RCCP-014 through RCCP-020 are platform-level presentation decisions for every model that uses a Harmonic Shaper; their wording is reusable while electrical networks remain model-specific.
- RCCP-004 supersedes the prototype framing, and RCCP-001 supersedes the Relay Current and Coupeville Relay Current names for this instrument.
- RCCP-005 did not define a serial prefix. The later `CVL26001` record and canonical site-organization policy control; the `CPC26001` mention on the Current model page is stale documentation to correct during promotion.
- RCCP-012 records the installed CVL26001 network values. A future reusable Current reference design may evolve through revisioned listening tests rather than making those values immutable for every Current.
- CRL-001 establishes the paired lipsticks as Reef's defining feature, not necessarily its dominant voice. CRL-002 now records HLL as a prototype layout: the owner is approximately 55% inclined to move the humbucker to the bridge but will decide only after testing and feedback from a musician.
- CRL-003 and CRL-014 record listening-confirmed descriptions of the current prototype. They should be reassessed if the humbucker moves.
- CRL-006, CRL-018, and CRL-022 describe one reusable two-branch passive architecture; CRL-019 records the failed A1M sweep, and CRL-020 and CRL-021 confirm that matching B1M pots fixed the mixing behavior. Lesser volume anomalies remain and may or may not justify changes after end-user testing, so the architecture is not yet fully validated as a reusable reference design.
- CRL-011 supersedes the early six-way, series-lipstick, bass-contour, and phase proposals for the simple baseline model.
- CRL-010 establishes a strong documentation boundary: Coupeville documentation describes committed designs and does not advertise future possibilities; Relay may explicitly encourage builder exploration and customization.
- CRL-013 is measured source data; CRL-014 is a listening-confirmed interpretation of the current prototype.
- CRL-015 is an approved measurement recommendation, not a publication requirement or cross-platform specification.
- CRL-024 is a qualified owner recollection, not a verified model rule: Reef is believed to have no treble-bleed network on either B1M branch volume.
