# Electric Hurdy-Gurdy Rev B4 Prototype Direction

- **Date:** 2026-10-04
- **Status:** Approved for physical testing; implementation exists as CAD, but the instrument is not built or validated.
- **Scope:** Internal engineering only. No site route, public asset, or publication approval.
- **Evidence:** [Owner conversation inventory](../engineering/extraction/sources/2026-10-04-hurdy-gurdy-design-conversation.md); [Rev B4 prototype record](../engineering/hurdy-gurdy/2026-10-04-rev-b4-prototype.md).

## Decision and reason

Use Rev B4 as the current print-and-fit test candidate. The wheel bows across the strings. The crank sits at the tail. A braced tuner support and anchored tail web are intended to carry string tension through the modular body. Keys project from the far edge, with the left hand reaching across a removable cover that spans every key. Tangents use short steel contact pins supported by printed bosses and metal inserts. The owner's measured GFS lipstick envelope guides removable pickup carriages.

These choices respond to the owner's corrections of the earlier wheel orientation, unreachable crank, weak tuner plate and anchors, misplaced keys, incomplete cover, and fragile tangents. The owner judged the revised design worth testing; this approval does not establish strength, playability, tuning stability, or sound.

## Next revision

Add a positive, serviceable key-travel stop. Set its final throw after measuring the Rev B4 key action, string contact, and return. The owner will provide other requested changes after testing. This requirement is **approved as a next-revision change**, not implemented in Rev B4.

## Supersedes

This direction supersedes the rejected aspects of earlier Rev B sketches and Rev B3: along-string wheel action, an inaccessible crank, the thin raised tuner sheet, near-side or exposed key arrangement, incomplete cover, and long exposed printed tangents. It does not supersede the still-useful goal of separately adjustable pickups.

## Open questions

- Does the printed structure hold tune under actual string tension over time, or need additional metal reinforcement?
- Which actual 2-left/2-right guitar tuners fit, and what are their bushing and anti-rotation dimensions?
- Which string gauges, pitch, patch, wheel surface, and bridge adjustments produce reliable bowing?
- What key return and adjustable tangent/intonation arrangement work after physical testing?
- What exact stopped key travel prevents excess tangent and string load while allowing reliable notes?
- Are three independently adjustable pickups useful after listening, and what placement gives good balance?
