# Decision: free DIY alternatives for K7RHY kits

- **Date:** 2026-10-25
- **Status:** Approved (principle). First application, the dummy-load stripboard, is **designed and modeled, not built or measured**.
- **Decided by:** the site owner.

## Decision

In addition to the kits K7RHY designs and sells, K7RHY publishes instructions for building the same circuit from common parts, so a builder does not have to buy from K7RHY. Each such alternative includes a stripboard layout so the builder does not need K7RHY's PCBs.

## Why

The owner's reason, in their words: so that you "don't need to buy the stuff" from K7RHY. The kit stays on sale; the alternative is an additional, free route to the same project.

## Scope as stated, and where it is open

- Stated for the kits K7RHY designs for sale. The only kit today is the 20W Dummy Load, the first application.
- **Open:** whether every future kit must have a DIY alternative before it goes on sale; whether the principle extends beyond ham-radio kits to guitar projects.
- **Open:** how a DIY alternative is validated before it is published. The first design is a model plus a layout; the engineering record proposes a measurement and thermal test before any numbers go public.

## First application

[`docs/engineering/dummy-load-stripboard/reference.md`](../engineering/dummy-load-stripboard/reference.md): a stripboard layout for the 20W Dummy Load with the same 50 Ω divider and power-measuring test pads. On 2026-10-25 the owner delegated two choices to the engineer: the diode (BAT46) and the power limits (10 W continuous, 20 W bursts with 3 W resistors; recommended pending a thermal test).

## Relationship to existing policy

[`docs/architecture/site-organization.md`](../architecture/site-organization.md) says K7RHY.app explains and presents while Shopify owns price, availability, inventory, orders, payment and checkout. A free build guide is explanatory content and does not conflict. That document has **not** been changed: where DIY alternatives appear in the site's organization is the next decision, and the canonical policy should be updated once it is made.

## Supersedes

Nothing.

## Unresolved

1. Where DIY alternatives appear on the site and how they relate to the kit's page and the Shop (under discussion, 2026-10-25).
2. Licence and attribution terms for published plans.
3. The scope questions above.
